## Summary

- The seven Freesound pages were accessible during the recorded checks and their HTML showed a Creative Commons 0 (CC0) marker. CC0 generally permits copying, modification, and redistribution; retain source-page and processing evidence.
- Four current videos are from Mixkit and were marked `videoFree`. The Video Free License lists permissions such as downloading, copying, modifying, and distributing. The User Terms also restrict similar or competing products/services. Applicability to this app and distribution through public Git/LFS and the APK has not been confirmed in writing by Mixkit.
- Three current videos are from Pexels. Their pages displayed a Free license, creator, and matching title when checked in a browser. Pexels terms restrict redistribution of standalone stock assets; the specific public-repository and APK packaging use still requires review under the full terms.
- All 14 items have recorded source-page or platform-license evidence. That does not confirm every redistribution method. Per-file status and hashes are in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
