# Third-Party License and Permission Record

Audit date: 2026-10-28. Scope: the seven audio tracks, seven videos, and Lucide leaf icon used by Nature Sleep. This record consolidates the license findings and permission questions from the prior license-audit documents. It is not legal advice and does not establish project-specific written permission.

## Summary

- **Freesound audio (7 items):** the recorded item pages returned HTTP 200 and their HTML showed a Creative Commons 0 (CC0) marker. CC0 generally permits copying, modification, and redistribution, including commercial use. The marker concerns the recording supplied by the uploader and does not resolve unrelated third-party rights. Keep the source-page and license evidence.
- **Mixkit video (4 items):** the recorded item pages were marked `videoFree`. The Video Free License lists permissions including downloading, copying, modifying, and distributing. Mixkit User Terms also restrict similar or competing products/services. No case-specific written response confirms how that restriction applies to this app or distribution through public Git/LFS and the APK.
- **Pexels video (3 items):** browser checks on 2026-10-28 recorded a Free label. Pexels terms restrict redistribution of standalone stock assets or use as a stock-media platform. Application to this public repository and APK packaging still needs review under the full terms.
- **Lucide leaf icon:** based on the Lucide `Leaf` icon, Lucide v1.8.0, under ISC. Preserve the relevant copyright and license notice. The icon is not an independently original work by the project author.

A platform license marker or a “pending” note is not itself a grant of permission. The project does not claim that all 14 media files have unconditional permission for every distribution method.
